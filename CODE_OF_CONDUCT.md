# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Bill Changelog welcoming and harassment-free for everyone.

Be respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, protect contributor and user privacy, and
assume good faith while remaining precise about evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and publication of private billing data are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the
community. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact methods on GitHub.

This policy is adapted from the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
